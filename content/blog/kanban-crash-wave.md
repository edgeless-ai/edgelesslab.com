The recorded crash share rose sharply for attempts started on October 8 and 9, then fell on October 10. The counts matter: **657 of 958** attempts had crash status on October 8, **446 of 607** on October 9, and **3 of 161** on October 10.

These are status counts from a snapshot, not a timeline of service availability or evidence of a particular cause or fix. Scrub the chart to see each day's numerator and denominator.
