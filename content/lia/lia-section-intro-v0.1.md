## What is LIA?

Latent Information Archaeology studies the intersections between fields: useful, defensible connections across bodies of knowledge.

LIA explores connections across science, mathematics, humanities, engineering, history, art, books, libraries, and institutions. A connection needs a mapping, prior art, disanalogies, a useful consequence, and a discriminating test. Shared vocabulary or model agreement does not establish a discovery.

This section collects work in development, not confirmed discoveries. The methods note considers the limits of inference in one area: reading artifacts. It does not define the scope of LIA. The creative demos are exploratory interfaces, not empirical validation. One concerns the software artist LIA, who is distinct from this research program.
