Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-06-26 · Edgeless Lab LLC

This policy explains what Edgeless Lab LLC ("Edgeless", "we") collects and how we use it when you use the Edgeless storefront.

What we collect

  • Order info: your name, shipping address, email, and phone — needed to make and ship your order.
  • Payment: processed by Stripe. We receive a confirmation and amount; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Design submissions: artwork you upload and its review results.
  • "Want it" votes: the email you provide to signal demand for a Bazaar design.
  • Basic technical data: e.g., IP address, used for security and abuse prevention.

How we use it

  • To process payments (Stripe) and manufacture + ship your order (our print partners, Printful and Printify).
  • To pay creator royalties (Stripe Connect).
  • To prevent spam, fraud, and abuse (including automated review of designs and demand signals).
  • To contact you about your order.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers needed to run the store: Stripe (payments + payouts), Printful and Printify (manufacturing + shipping), and infrastructure providers (Cloudflare, Render). We do not sell your personal information.

Retention & your choices

We keep order and tax records as required by law, and other data only as long as needed to run the service. You may request access to or deletion of your personal data by emailing us (subject to records we must legally retain).

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

Edgeless Lab LLC does not sell your personal information, and does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). Because we do not sell or share, there is nothing to opt out of — but you may still contact us at souls@edgelesslab.com with the subject "Do Not Sell or Share" to confirm your preference on record.

Your California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you have the right to: know what personal information we collect and how we use it; request access to or a copy of it; request correction or deletion; and not receive discriminatory treatment for exercising these rights. To exercise any of these, email souls@edgelesslab.com. We will verify your request (typically via the email associated with your order) and respond within the timeframes required by law. You may use an authorized agent to submit a request on your behalf.

Responsible disclosure

Found a security issue? Email souls@edgelesslab.com with details and we'll work with you in good faith. Please don't access other users' data or disrupt the service while testing.
