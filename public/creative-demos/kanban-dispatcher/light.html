Model, sources & limits

A local illustrative state machine: the page never starts workers or changes a board. Historical snapshot counts are dated October 11, 2026, not current telemetry. Geometry and timing are authored illustration values. Parent readiness and explicit block/unblock transitions come from the source model.

Dependency semantics were traced in the installed Hermes hermes_cli/kanban_db.py; dated board counts came from a read-only SELECT status, count(*) FROM tasks GROUP BY status snapshot. The moving cards and tray readouts come from the embedded model, not those historical totals.
